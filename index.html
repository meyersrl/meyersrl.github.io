Home Assistant

A private, self-hosted smart home hub running on a Raspberry Pi in a single household.

What this application does

This is a personal installation of Home Assistant, the open-source home automation platform. It runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 inside one private home and is used by the members of that household only. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

Why it connects to Google

The owner of this installation uses Google Nest thermostats, cameras, and a video doorbell. This application uses the Google Smart Device Management API so those devices can be viewed and controlled from the household dashboard, alongside their other smart home equipment.

  • Read and adjust Nest thermostat temperature, mode, and fan settings
  • View live camera and doorbell streams inside the home
  • Receive motion, person, and doorbell events to trigger household automations

Where the data goes

Nowhere. All device data stays on the Raspberry Pi in the home where it is installed. Nothing is transmitted to any third party, published, or sold. See the Privacy Policy for details.
